CONSTRUCTION INSPECTION GUIDELINES
Arizona Jurisdictions - Reference Guide
Document Reference: INSP-2026-01

================================================================
SECTION 1: GENERAL INSPECTION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

1.1 Purpose
Inspections ensure that construction work complies with the approved plans and applicable building codes. All permitted work must be inspected at the required stages before proceeding to subsequent phases of construction.

1.2 Inspection Scheduling
- Inspections must be requested at least 24 hours in advance
- The contractor or property owner is responsible for scheduling inspections
- Work must be accessible and ready for inspection at the scheduled time
- If work is not ready, the inspection may be cancelled and rescheduled

1.3 Required Documentation at Inspection
The following must be available on-site during any inspection:
- Approved permit and plans (job copy)
- Special inspection reports (if applicable)
- Material test reports (concrete, steel, soils)
- Product data sheets and submittals
- Fire-rated assembly documentation

SECTION 2: BUILDING INSPECTIONS
================================================================

2.1 Foundation Inspection
Timing: After excavation and before concrete placement
Verify:
- Footing dimensions match approved plans
- Rebar size, spacing, and placement per structural drawings
- Soil bearing conditions per geotechnical report
- Anchor bolt placement and embedment per plans
- Form alignment and bracing
- Clean excavation (no loose soil, standing water, or debris)

2.2 Underground Utility Inspection
Timing: Before backfill of underground utilities
Verify:
- Pipe material, size, and slope per plumbing plans
- Proper bedding and backfill materials
- Pipe joints and connections
- Thrust blocks and restraints
- Separation from other utilities per code

2.3 Slab Inspection
Timing: After vapor barrier, rebar, and embedded items but before concrete pour
Verify:
- Vapor barrier properly installed (minimum 10 mil)
- Slab reinforcement per structural plans
- Post-tension tendon layout (if applicable)
- Embedded conduit and plumbing properly located
- Edge forms at correct elevation
- Expansion joints properly located

2.4 Framing Inspection
Timing: After framing is complete but before insulation and drywall
Verify:
- Member sizes and spacing per structural plans
- Connections per structural details
- Hold-downs and straps properly installed
- Proper nailing patterns for shear walls
- Headers and beams properly sized and supported
- Fire blocking installed per code
- Draft stopping in concealed spaces
- Rough openings correctly sized and located

2.5 Insulation Inspection
Timing: After insulation installation but before drywall
Verify:
- Insulation type and R-value per energy compliance documents
- Proper installation without gaps, voids, or compression
- Vapor barrier orientation (if required)
- Air sealing at penetrations
- Compliance with energy code requirements

2.6 Final Building Inspection
Timing: When all work is complete and building is ready for occupancy
Verify:
- All previous inspections passed
- All work matches approved plans
- Means of egress clear and properly marked
- Accessibility features installed per plans
- Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors installed and functional
- Address numbers posted and visible
- Site work complete (grading, drainage, sidewalks)

================================================================
SECTION 3: MECHANICAL (HVAC) INSPECTIONS
================================================================

3.1 Underground Mechanical
Timing: Before backfill of underground mechanical work
Verify:
- Ground-source heat pump loops (if applicable)
- Underground ductwork (if applicable)
- Condensate drain piping

3.2 Rough-In Inspection
Timing: After ductwork and piping installed, before concealment
Verify:
- Duct material, sizes, and routing per mechanical plans
- Duct support and hanger spacing per code
- Fire and smoke dampers at rated barriers
- Access panels provided per code
- Refrigerant piping properly supported and insulated
- Condensate drain piping properly sloped
- Control wiring installed

3.3 Equipment Installation Inspection
Timing: After HVAC equipment is set in place
Verify:
- Equipment matches approved schedule (manufacturer, model, capacity)
- Proper structural support (especially rooftop units)
- Clearances for maintenance access
- Electrical connections per electrical plans
- Refrigerant piping connections
- Condensate drain connections
- Vibration isolation (if required)
- Seismic bracing (if required)

3.4 Controls and Balancing Inspection
Timing: After system startup and air balancing
Verify:
- Controls operating per sequence of operations
- Thermostat locations and setpoints
- Air balancing report (TAB report)
- Economizer operation (if applicable)
- Demand control ventilation (if applicable)
- Energy recovery ventilation (if applicable)

3.5 Final Mechanical Inspection
Timing: When all mechanical work is complete
Verify:
- All previous inspections passed
- System operating properly
- Commissioning report submitted (if required)
- All access panels labeled and accessible
- Equipment labels installed showing rated capacity

================================================================
SECTION 4: ELECTRICAL INSPECTIONS
================================================================

4.1 Underground Electrical
Timing: Before backfill of underground electrical work
Verify:
- Conduit material, size, and burial depth per NEC
- Proper bends and fittings
- Grounding electrode system
- Pull wire or rope in conduits

4.2 Rough-In Inspection
Timing: After wiring installed, before concealment
Verify:
- Wire sizes match panel schedule
- Proper box sizes for number of conductors
- GFCI and AFCI protection where required by NEC 2023
- Grounding and bonding per NEC Article 250
- Proper support and securing of wiring methods
- Fire-rated penetrations properly sealed

4.3 Service Entrance Inspection
Timing: After meter base and service equipment installed
Verify:
- Service entrance conductor sizing per NEC
- Main overcurrent protection rating
- Grounding electrode conductor and connections
- Meter base location and clearances
- Working clearances per NEC Article 110

4.4 Final Electrical Inspection
Timing: When all electrical work is complete
Verify:
- All previous inspections passed
- Panel schedules match installed conditions
- All circuits properly labeled
- GFCI devices tested and functional
- AFCI devices tested and functional
- Smoke detector interconnection verified
- Emergency lighting operational
- Exit signs properly located and illuminated

================================================================
SECTION 5: FIRE SAFETY INSPECTIONS
================================================================

5.1 Fire Suppression System Inspection
Timing: After installation of sprinkler system
Verify:
- Sprinkler layout per approved NFPA 13 design
- Pipe sizes and hanger spacing per NFPA 13
- Head type and temperature rating per design
- Proper coverage areas maintained
- Fire department connection (FDC) installed and accessible
- Hydrostatic test completed (200 PSI for 2 hours)

5.2 Fire Alarm System Inspection
Timing: After installation of fire alarm system
Verify:
- Device locations per approved plans
- Notification appliance coverage per NFPA 72
- Circuit supervision
- Battery backup adequate
- System functional test (point-to-point)
- Monitoring connection verified

================================================================
SECTION 6: INSPECTION RESULTS
================================================================

6.1 Possible Results
- PASS: Work complies with approved plans and codes
- CONDITIONAL PASS: Minor items to correct, work may proceed
- FAIL: Work does not comply, corrections required, re-inspection needed
- NOT READY: Work not accessible or not complete enough for inspection

6.2 Failed Inspections
When an inspection fails:
- Inspector will document specific deficiencies in writing
- Contractor must correct all deficiencies
- Contractor must request a re-inspection
- No work may proceed beyond the failed inspection point
- Repeated failures may result in a stop-work order

6.3 Re-Inspection Fees
Most jurisdictions charge a re-inspection fee after the first failed inspection:
- Typical fee: $75-$150 per re-inspection
- Fees must be paid before the re-inspection will be scheduled
