STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS FOR CONSTRUCTION PERMITS
Arizona Jurisdictions - Reference Guide
Document Reference: STR-2026-01

================================================================
SECTION 1: GENERAL STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

1.1 Design Standards
All structural designs must comply with:
- International Building Code (IBC) 2021 as adopted by the applicable Arizona jurisdiction
- American Concrete Institute (ACI) 318-19 for concrete design
- American Institute of Steel Construction (AISC) 360-22 for steel design
- American Wood Council (AWC) NDS-2018 for wood design
- Masonry Standards Joint Committee (MSJC) TMS 402/602 for masonry design

1.2 Licensed Professional Requirement
All structural drawings and calculations must be prepared by, or under the direct supervision of, a structural engineer registered in the State of Arizona. The engineer's stamp and signature must appear on all structural drawing sheets and the cover of all calculation packages.

1.3 Soil Investigation
A geotechnical investigation report is required for:
- All commercial and multi-family residential projects
- Single-family residential projects on slopes greater than 10%
- Projects in known problem soil areas (expansive soils, collapsible soils)
- Projects requiring deep foundations

The geotechnical report must include:
- Soil boring logs with classifications
- Laboratory test results
- Recommended bearing pressures
- Foundation recommendations
- Earthwork recommendations
- Seismic site classification per ASCE 7

SECTION 2: FOUNDATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

2.1 Minimum Foundation Requirements
All foundations must be designed to:
- Support all applicable loads without exceeding allowable soil bearing pressure
- Extend below frost line (not typically a concern in Arizona, but minimum 12 inches below grade)
- Provide adequate anchorage for the structure above
- Include provisions for expansion/contraction in expansive soil conditions

2.2 Foundation Plan Requirements
The foundation plan must show:
- All footing locations with dimensions, depths, and reinforcement
- Pier and grade beam locations (if applicable)
- Anchor bolt layout with edge distances and embedment depths
- Slab-on-grade thickness, reinforcement, and joint layout
- Post-tension tendon layout (if applicable)
- Special inspection requirements

2.3 Commercial Foundation Requirements
Commercial buildings additionally require:
- Continuous footings under all bearing walls
- Isolated spread footings under columns
- Combined footings or mat foundations where required by soil conditions
- Basement wall design for lateral earth pressure (if applicable)
- Waterproofing details for below-grade construction

================================================================
SECTION 3: STRUCTURAL FRAMING REQUIREMENTS
================================================================

3.1 Floor Framing
Floor framing plans must indicate:
- Member sizes and spacing
- Connection details
- Beam and header schedules
- Opening reinforcement details
- Diaphragm sheathing specifications
- Load path for concentrated loads

3.2 Roof Framing
Roof framing plans must show:
- Rafter or truss sizes and spacing
- Ridge beam specifications
- Valley and hip details
- Roof diaphragm specifications
- Parapet bracing (if applicable)
- Equipment support framing for rooftop units

3.3 Rooftop Mechanical Unit Supports
For buildings with rooftop mechanical equipment:
- Structural support must be designed for the operating weight plus seismic forces
- Support framing must include curb details showing attachment to roof structure
- Load calculations must account for:
  * Equipment dead weight (operating weight including refrigerant)
  * Wind uplift forces on equipment
  * Seismic forces per ASCE 7 Chapter 13
  * Vibration isolation provisions
- Connection details from equipment support to primary roof structure must be provided
- Load path must be traced from equipment support through the structure to the foundation
- Equipment weighing more than 400 pounds requires engineered support design
- All rooftop units must have housekeeping pads or structural curbs
- Vibration isolation must be designed to prevent structural resonance

Note: Phoenix Mechanical Code Section 301.5 requires structural drawings for all rooftop units weighing over 2,000 lbs. These drawings must be stamped by a licensed structural engineer.

SECTION 4: LATERAL FORCE RESISTING SYSTEM
================================================================

4.1 Wind Design
Wind design must comply with:
- ASCE 7-22 Chapter 26-31
- Basic wind speed for most Arizona locations: 115 mph (Ultimate)
- Exposure category based on site conditions
- Components and cladding pressures for exterior envelope design

4.2 Seismic Design
Seismic design must comply with:
- ASCE 7-22 Chapters 11-23
- Seismic design category per site-specific analysis
- Most Arizona locations: Seismic Design Category B
- Some locations near faults may require higher categories

4.3 Required Lateral System Documentation
The structural drawings must show:
- Complete lateral force resisting system
- Shear wall schedule with hold-down and anchor bolt specifications
- Moment frame design (if applicable)
- Braced frame design (if applicable)
- Drag strut and collector design
- Diaphragm design and connections
- Foundation design for overturning and sliding

================================================================
SECTION 5: SPECIAL INSPECTIONS
================================================================

5.1 Required Special Inspections
The following work requires special inspection per IBC Chapter 17:
- Concrete placement and testing
- Structural steel welding
- High-strength bolting
- Masonry construction
- Post-installed anchors
- Spray-applied fireproofing
- Structural wood (engineered products)

5.2 Statement of Special Inspections
A Statement of Special Inspections must be submitted with the permit application for:
- All commercial projects
- Multi-family residential projects
- Single-family projects with unusual structural systems

The statement must identify:
- All special inspections required
- The special inspection agency to be used
- The structural observations schedule
